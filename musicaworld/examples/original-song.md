## 8. Production Direction

- **Sonic aesthetic**: lo-fi tape — warm, slightly saturated, never harsh.
- **Space**: small room reverb on vocals (0.8s decay), larger hall on strings (2.5s); spring reverb send on guitar (0.5s, dub-style).
- **Low-end strategy**: upright bass owns 60–120Hz in verses; Rhodes high-passed at 120Hz to avoid mud.
- **Texture**: constant tape hiss at −30dB, vinyl crackle sparse; no digital noise.
- **Loudness target**: ≈ −14 LUFS integrated (streaming).
- **Reference palette**: "Jakarta indie-folk scene, 2019–2023" — characteristics: tape saturation, close-mic intimacy, bossa-nova rhythm, Indonesian-language indie.
