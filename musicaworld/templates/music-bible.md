## 12. Continuity Rules

These are inviolable — check before delivering any new track:

1. **Motif integrity**: every track uses ≥1 Bible motif; transformations
   must follow the stated rules.
2. **Timbre consistency**: no instrument enters without Palette
   justification. No stock presets that contradict the aesthetic.
3. **Harmonic grammar**: chord choices must be derivable from §6.
4. **Emotional vocabulary**: the track's emotion must exist in §4.
5. **Rhythmic signature**: the groove must match §7.
6. **Production uniformity**: same reverb space, low-end policy,
   loudness target across all tracks.
7. **No repetition**: a motif may not appear in two tracks with
   identical arrangement — it must transform or stay home.
8. **Rejection log**: if a concept is killed, record it in §8 with
   reason — do not silently retry.
