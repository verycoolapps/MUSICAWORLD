## 4. Lyrics

> Rules: specific imagery over abstraction; natural language; mixed
> rhyme (exact/near/slant/meter-only); stress mapped to strong beats;
> each verse adds information, chorus pays it off; no filler hook loops.

```
[INTRO]

[VERSE 1]

[PRE-CHORUS]

[CHORUS]

[VERSE 2]

[PRE-CHORUS 2]

[CHORUS 2]

[BRIDGE]

[FINAL CHORUS]

[OUTRO]
```

- Lyric hook phrase: (≤8 syllables, concrete image or thesis)
- Verse 2 variation: (what new information does it carry?)
- Bridge lyric job: (perspective shift? confession? silence?)
