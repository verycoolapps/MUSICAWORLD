# Music Theory Reference

Depth file for harmony, rhythm, melody, and the emotion→device map.
Load when composing anything that needs structural decisions.

## Scales & Tonality

- **Major/minor**: basis of tonal music; minor variants (natural, harmonic,
  melodic, Dorian, Phrygian, Locrian) change color more than darkness.
- **Modes**: Ionian, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Aeolian, Locrian.
  Dorian = minor with raised 6th (hopeful); Lydian = major with #4 (airy,
  "floating"); Phrygian = dark, Spanish/metal; Mixolydian = dominant, rock/blues.
- **Chromatic & atonal**: chromatic approach notes, tritones, clusters;
  pitch-class sets, serialism, spectral harmony (chords built from overtone
  series of a fundamental).
- **Microtonality**: quarter tones, 19/24-TET, just intonation ratios
  (3:2, 5:4, 7:4). Use when the brief calls for "otherworldly," "ancient,"
  or "unsettlingly familiar but wrong."

## Harmony

- **Progressions**: I–V–vi–IV (pop), ii–V–I (jazz), i–VI–III–VII (epic minor),
  modal vamps (single chord + melody interest). Avoid default progressions
  unless motivated.
- **Extensions**: 9ths, 11ths, 13ths add color; sus chords suspend resolution;
  add9/add11 for airiness; altered dominants (b9, #9, b13) for tension.
- **Inversions & voice leading**: keep common tones, minimize leaps in inner
  voices, resolve tendency tones (7th down, #4 up, tritone inward).
- **Modal interchange**: borrow chords from parallel modes (iv in major,
  bVII, bVI) — instant cinematic color.
- **Dissonance strategy**: tension must have a release plan. Cluster → open
  fifth, tritone → unison, polychord → single chord. Never wallow.
- **Metric modulation**: pivot note value (e.g., eighth = new quarter) to
  shift tempo feel without changing pulse drastically.

## Rhythm & Meter

- **Common**: 4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 12/8. **Asymmetric**: 5/4 (heroic unease),
  7/8 (limping/propulsive), additive rhythms (2+2+3). Use asymmetric meters
  when they serve groove or anxiety — not as decoration.
- **Polyrhythm**: 3-against-4, 5-against-4; assign layers cleanly so each
  part is independently coherent. Specify which layer is "the beat."
- **Syncopation & groove**: push the "and" of beats; anticipate downbeats by
  16ths. Specify swing/shuffle as straight %, not vibes.
- **Tempo map**: give BPM per section; rit./accel. marked explicitly.

## Melody

- **Motif**: 2–5 note cell, rhythmically distinctive. Develop via
  transposition, inversion, augmentation, fragmentation, sequence.
- **Contour**: arch (rise–peak–fall) = classic lyricism; wave = restless;
  descending = resignation; leap + step = drama.
- **Range & tessitura**: keep hooks in singable range (roughly octave+).
- **Memorability test**: can a listener hum it after one listen? If the
  motif can't survive abstraction (hum it, strip harmony), simplify.

## Emotion → Device Map

| Emotion | Harmony | Rhythm | Timbre | Register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Joy | major, add9, I–V–vi–IV | upbeat, syncopated | bright, plucked, airy | mid-high |
| Nostalgia | maj7, ii–V, borrowed iv | mid tempo, laid-back | warm, vinyl-ish | mid |
| Sadness | minor, ii–V–i, sus | slow, space between | soft, close-mic | low-mid |
| Bittersweet | minor + major lift (Picardy) | rubato feel | piano, strings | mid |
| Epic | i–VI–III–VII, pedal tones | driving 8ths | low strings, brass, taiko | full range |
| Dark | phrygian, tritone, clusters | slow pulse, sudden hits | low drones, detuned | low |
| Magical | lydian, whole-tone, maj7#11 | floating, no strict pulse | celesta, glock, shimmer | high |
| Futuristic | quartal, synthetic scales | mechanical, quantized | FM, granular, sub | extreme |
| Tension | tritone, dominant sus, ostinato | accelerating ostinato | pulsing low strings | rising |

This map is a starting vocabulary, not a rulebook — deliberately violate it
with reason (e.g., "sad" as frantic major-key punk = panic-grief).

## Complexity Budget

Match theory density to context: radio pop needs clean loops; trailer needs
one massive gesture; ambient needs texture over progression. State the
complexity tier in your spec: **Minimal / Standard / Dense / Experimental**.
