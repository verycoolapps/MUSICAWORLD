# Experimental Music Reference

Load for avant-garde, noise, drone, spectral, microtonal, and
boundary-pushing work. Experimental ≠ random — structure and intent
are mandatory.

## Experimental Territories

- **Drone**: sustained tones, slow beating (detune 1–10 cents creates
  movement), harmonic evolution via filtering, stacked fundamentals
  + overtones. Think Eliane Radigue, Tim Hecker.
- **Noise music**: texture-first; specify noise color (white/pink/brown),
  filtering, amplitude behavior. White noise = harsh; brown = sub-heavy.
- **Spectral composition**: chords/instruments tuned to a fundamental's
  overtone series; inharmonic partials for metallic/alien feel.
- **Microtonality**: 19-TET, 31-TET, just intonation; specify interval
  sizes in cents when relevant (100 cents = semitone).
- **Glitch**: digital artifacts as material — bit reduction, sample-rate
  down, buffer errors, granular skips. Quantize the chaos: pattern the
  corruption.
- **Electroacoustic/acousmatic**: source recording transformed beyond
  recognition; acousmatic = heard without seeing source.
- **Field recording composition**: environment as instrument; layer
  manipulated field captures under composed structure.
- **Silence & negative space**: absence as material — specify durations
  and what the listener should notice.

## Structure Without Convention

Experimental works still need architecture. Options:
- **Process pieces**: document the process ("ring modulated at 0.1Hz
  sweep over 20min") — the process IS the form.
- **Textural arcs**: density/dynamics maps instead of chord progressions.
- **Indeterminate frames**: bounded parameters, performer/system freedom
  inside (e.g., "any cluster within this register, entries 5–20s apart").
- **Found + composed**: concrete sound sources placed in composed time.

## Timbre as Primary Material

When melody/harmony recede, specify:
- Spectral envelope (what frequencies dominate over time)
- Amplitude behavior (steady, pulsing, chaotic, breathing)
- Spatial motion (static, orbiting, collapsing)
- Morphing trajectory (A→B over duration)

## Quality Control for the Weird

Experimental output still passes the gate: is there an identity? Does it
evolve? Is the strangeness *chosen*? Could someone listen for 10 minutes?
Weirdness needs patience budget: state intended listening duration.

## Anti-Patterns

- Random noise dump with no process.
- "Experimental" as excuse for unfinished structure.
- Unbounded indeterminacy — even chaos needs edges.
- Ignoring psychoacoustics: extreme high/low frequencies and dense
  clusters cause fatigue; design for listenability.
