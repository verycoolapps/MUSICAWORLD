## ✨ What Is MusicaWorld?

**MusicaWorld** is a premium AI-agent skill that turns your agent into a
multidisciplinary creative intelligence: **composer, film scorer, sound
designer, producer, music theorist, and sonic worldbuilder** — all at once.

The philosophy is simple and strict: **the future of music is not more of
what exists — it is discovering what music has not yet become.** Every
unusual decision must have a reason. Complexity ≠ quality. Randomness ≠
originality. Loudness ≠ impact.

It does not hand you a vague mood board. It hands you **production-ready
specifications**: real chords, real motifs, real lyrics, real arrangements,
and prompts tuned for Suno, Udio, Stable Audio, ElevenLabs, and DAWs.

## 🏛️ How It Works

Every request runs through a fixed creative pipeline:

```
UNDERSTAND → DISCOVER → EXPLORE → CREATE → GATE → REFINE → DELIVER
```

1. **Understand** — extract purpose, emotion, audience, duration,
   vocal/instrumental, platform, production constraints.
2. **Discover** — at most one round of questions, and only for
   genuinely missing material info. Rich brief? It creates
   immediately.
3. **Explore** — when direction is open, offers **3 genuinely
   different directions** — distinct emotional identities, not
   reskins of one idea.
4. **Create** — produces a complete production-ready specification.
   Real musical content. Never `[melody here]`.
5. **Gate** — runs the Anti-AI-Slop Quality Gate (§ below). Scores
   honestly.
6. **Refine** — fixes weaknesses without destroying identity.
7. **Deliver** — structured output in exactly the requested format.

### 🧠 Creative Memory — The Music Bible

For any project with **3+ tracks or multi-session continuity**,
MusicaWorld maintains a **Music Bible**: motif library, instrument
palette, harmonic/rhythmic language, emotional vocabulary, track
catalog, rejected concepts. It will never accidentally repeat motifs
or timbres inside one project.

## ⚖️ The Anti-AI-Slop Quality Gate

Before anything is delivered, every concept is scored on **100 points**.
This is non-negotiable — the score is shown, not hidden.

| Dimension | Pts | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Originality | 20 | Distinctive decisions, not clichés? |
| Musicality | 15 | Theory coherent, progression intentional? |
| Emotional Impact | 15 | Arc convincing, earned climax? |
| Melodic Identity | 10 | Memorable, singable hook/motif? |
| Arrangement | 10 | Evolves, layers purposeful, transitions clean? |
| Sound Design | 10 | Textures distinctive, spectrum managed? |
| Production | 10 | Mix/master coherent, format-ready? |
| Memorability | 5 | Fingerprint recognizable? |
| Context Fit | 5 | Right for stated use? |

| Score | Verdict |
|---:|---|
| **95+** | 🏆 Masterpiece |
| **90–94** | ✨ Exceptional |
| **85–89** | 🔥 High quality |
| **75–84** | 🔧 Refine |
| **< 75** | ♻️ Rework — weakest dimensions named, concrete fixes proposed |

## 🏛️ Cara Kerjanya

Setiap permintaan melewati alur kreatif yang tetap:

```
Pahami → Jelajahi Info → Eksplorasi → Cipta → Gerbang → Perbaiki → Serahkan
```

1. **Pahami** — ekstrak tujuan, emosi, audiens, durasi, vokal/
   instrumental, platform, batasan produksi.
2. **Jelajahi Info** — maksimal satu ronde pertanyaan, dan hanya untuk
   info material yang benar-benar hilang. Brief sudah kaya? Langsung
   berkarya.
3. **Eksplorasi** — saat arah masih terbuka, menawarkan **3 arah yang
   benar-benar berbeda** — identitas emosional yang berbeda, bukan
   reskin dari satu ide.
4. **Cipta** — menghasilkan spesifikasi siap-produksi yang utuh.
   Konten musik nyata. Tidak pernah `[melodi di sini]`.
5. **Gerbang** — menjalankan Gerbang Kualitas Anti-AI-Slop (lihat
   bawah). Skor jujur.
6. **Perbaiki** — memperbaiki kelemahan tanpa menghancurkan identitas.
7. **Serahkan** — output terstruktur persis dalam format yang diminta.
